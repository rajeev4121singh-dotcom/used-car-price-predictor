# Added features

- Historical purchase price lookup by brand/model/purchase year from user-provided CSV.
- Manual original invoice price and owner asking price.
- Insurance, accident/flood damage, service history, condition, tyres, repaint and modification inputs.
- Richer vehicle specs where source data has them: mileage, engine, max power, seats.
- Clear separation of ML estimate and manually configured condition scenario adjustments.

The baseline CarDekho data does not contain actual invoices or condition/insurance histories.
